GOVERNANCE & CONSUMER RIGHTS

CONSUMER RIGHTS & STORE POLICY

ORDON strictly complies with Egyptian Consumer Protection Law No. 181/2018 and its executive regulations to ensure complete trust and transparency:

Try-On & Inspection on Delivery:

You have the legal right to inspect and try on your items in the presence of the courier to verify size, fabric, and style before paying.

Exchange & Return Window:

You can return or exchange any unworn product with original tags attached within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or direct exchange.

Manufacturing Defect Warranty:

In case of any manufacturing flaw in fabric, stitching, or dyeing, the item will be replaced immediately free of charge or refunded in full.
