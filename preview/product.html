CONSUMER RIGHTS (LAW 181/2018)

Official Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency Compliance

Open-Box Try-On Right:

You are legally authorized to examine the package in front of our courier to verify design, fabric quality, and sizing before making cash payment.

14-Day Exchange & Refund Window:

Under Article 17 of Law 181, you can return or exchange any item within 14 calendar days from delivery date with original tags attached.

30-Day Manufacturing Flaw Warranty:

Under Article 21, any product with a confirmed stitching or manufacturing defect is eligible for full replacement or cash refund within 30 days without fees.
